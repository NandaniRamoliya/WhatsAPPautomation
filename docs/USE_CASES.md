# 100 Businesses – Which Preset & Settings to Use

Every business below runs on this kit **without new code**. Pick the preset in the setup wizard, then adjust the
listed settings. Modules: 🛒 Ordering · 📅 Booking · ❓ Enquiry (FAQs + lead form).

**Fast-start picks for your first 30 days:** kirana, milk booth, pharmacy, bakery, restaurant, laundry, salon,
gas agency, RO water supplier and tiffin service – frequent repeat orders and clear flows, so results show quickly.
