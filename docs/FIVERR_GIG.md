## 4. FAQ (paste each)

**Do you use the official WhatsApp API?**
Yes – Meta's official WhatsApp Business Cloud API. No unofficial tools that get numbers banned.

**Can I keep using my current WhatsApp number?**
Usually yes. Meta lets many WhatsApp Business app numbers also connect to the API, so you can keep replying from your phone – the bot automatically goes quiet in a chat when you reply yourself. Otherwise we use a new number. I check what works for you first.

**What will I pay apart from my order?**
Meta charges for template messages (sent after 24 hours) by category and country; replies within 24 hours of the customer's message are free. Hosting is a small monthly server cost. The optional AI uses your Anthropic (Claude) account – roughly ₹0.5–2 per AI reply depending on how much information it reads (buttons, FAQs and menus cost nothing). A cheaper model can be chosen for high volume. I estimate your costs before starting.

**What do I need to provide?**
Your business details, a Facebook/Meta Business account (I guide you), a phone number for the bot, your FAQs, and your product list or services. For the AI: your price list, menu, policies or website.

**Which languages does the bot speak?**
English, Hindi, Gujarati and Hinglish are built in – customers can pick their language. The AI replies in whatever language the customer writes.

**Can my team reply manually?**
Yes. "Talk to us" hands the chat to your team in a shared inbox (or from your phone); the bot pauses for that customer.

**Which CRMs can it connect to?**
Google Sheets, HubSpot and Airtable directly; Zoho, Pipedrive, GoHighLevel and any CRM with an API through n8n, Make or Zapier.

**Can it send bulk messages?**
Only to customers who opted in, using Meta-approved templates. This keeps your number safe.

**Do I own the bot?**
Yes. It runs in your own accounts. The Premium package also includes the source code.

**How long does Meta approval take?**
Message templates are usually approved within minutes to a day. Business verification can take a few days, but the bot can go live before it finishes.

## 7. Delivery playbook per package

All packages start the same: **Agency dashboard → + New client business → choose preset**, then the setup wizard.
Detailed steps: `docs/CLIENT_ONBOARDING.md` and the Developer Guide PDF (Part D).

### Basic – Smart Menu Bot (target: 2–3 hours of work)
- [ ] Business created, preset "Other / Custom" (or closest), Setup wizard done
- [ ] **Menu & quick replies**: 3–6 buttons (Price list PDF, Offers, Location, Timings, Website…)
- [ ] **FAQs**: up to 15 with keywords (English + Hindi/Gujarati words)
- [ ] Bot messages tuned to the business's tone; language choice if needed
- [ ] WhatsApp connected (Settings → WhatsApp), templates submitted
- [ ] Tested on the buyer's number: Hi, every button, 5 typed FAQs, "talk to someone", STOP
- [ ] Delivery message: dashboard link + login, User Guide PDF, 3–5 minute walkthrough video

### Standard – AI Assistant + Leads (target: 1 day)
Everything in Basic, plus:
- [ ] **AI assistant**: ANTHROPIC_API_KEY on the server, AI on, tone + assistant name + owner instructions
- [ ] **Knowledge base**: price list/menu/policies (PDF or text) + 2–5 website pages; "Try a question" with 10 real questions
- [ ] **Enquiry form** with the buyer's lead questions
- [ ] **Integrations**: Google Sheets (Apps Script) or HubSpot / Airtable / webhook → **Send test** ✅ → real test lead
- [ ] Delivery video shows a lead arriving in the sheet/CRM

### Premium – Complete Sales / Booking System (target: 1.5–2 days)
Everything in Standard, plus **one** of:
- **Sales**: Products (CSV import), delivery/pickup rules, payments (UPI / Razorpay / Stripe / link), order status flow tested end-to-end
- **Booking**: Services (aftercare + recall days), staff + hours + leave, triage questions, reminders, check-in → Google review
- [ ] Message templates approved; broadcast template for offers (opted-in only)
- [ ] Team logins (owner + staff), go-live checklist on Home fully ticked
- [ ] Hosted on the buyer's own server/Render (if purchased) or your agency server
- [ ] Source code: repository access or zip + Developer Guide PDF

### After delivery (gets you 5-star reviews)
- Day 1: send the walkthrough video and the User Guide PDF
- Day 3: "How are customers using it?" – fix any FAQ/text gaps for free
- Day 7: ask for the review; offer the monthly support extra
