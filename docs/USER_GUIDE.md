# User Guide – Your WhatsApp Assistant

This guide is for **business owners and staff**. No technical knowledge needed.
Your assistant answers customers on WhatsApp 24 hours a day – it takes orders, books appointments, answers common
questions, and tells you whenever a person is needed. You manage everything from one website: **the dashboard**.

## 1. How it works in one minute

1. A customer scans your QR code or sends "Hi" to your WhatsApp business number.
2. The assistant replies **instantly** with buttons: *Order now*, *Book appointment*, *Questions*, *Talk to us*…
3. The customer taps buttons – no typing, no app to install.
4. When an order or booking is made, **your phone gets a WhatsApp alert** and the dashboard **beeps**.
5. You update the order or appointment with one tap – the customer is told automatically.
6. Reminders, thank-you messages, review requests and "time for your next visit" messages go out **on their own**.
7. If a customer wants a human, the bot steps aside and you chat from the dashboard.

## 2. Logging in

Open the dashboard link your developer gave you (for example `https://bot.yourshop.com`) and sign in with your
email and password.

* Works on a computer, tablet or phone. On a phone, tap **☰** (top left) to open the menu.
* Change your password: **Settings → My account**.
* Forgot your password? Ask your developer/agency to reset it.

**Tip:** keep the dashboard open on the shop/reception computer all day. Tap anywhere on it once after opening –
browsers only allow the alert **sound** after you have clicked on the page.

## 3. First-time setup (wizard)

The first time you log in, a 5-step wizard opens (you can reopen it from **Setup wizard** in the menu):

1. **Business type** – choose the card closest to your business (Grocery, Restaurant, Pharmacy, Clinic, Dental,
   Salon, Gym, Coaching, Home services, Real estate, Other). Leave "Add example products / services / FAQs" on to
   see how everything works – you can edit or delete the examples later.
2. **Details** – business name, **owner WhatsApp number** (alerts go here), address, Google Maps link,
   Google review link, bot language (English or Hinglish), time zone.
3. **Opening hours** – switch each day on or off and set times. Add a break (e.g. 1:30–5:00 PM lunch) with
   "+ add break / 2nd shift".
4. **How it works** – delivery fee, free delivery limit, minimum order, payment options, UPI ID (shops) or booking
   rules (clinics/salons).
5. **Test & go live** – a checklist of what is left. Click **Finish setup**.

## 4. The Home page

* **Today's numbers** – orders, sales, appointments, no-shows, chats waiting for you, new enquiries, customers.
* **Red "Chats waiting for you"** – somebody asked for a person. Click *Open chats*.
* **Yellow warning about undelivered messages** – usually means a message template is needed (see §16) or WhatsApp
  is not connected yet.
* **Go-live checklist** – every step to be fully live. Click *Do it →* next to anything not ticked.
* **Last 7 days** – small charts of orders, appointments and people who messaged.
* **Share your WhatsApp link** – your QR code and chat link. Click **Download big QR (PNG)** and print it for your
  counter, bills, bags, visiting cards and posters. Post the chat link on Instagram, Facebook and your
  Google Business Profile.

## 5. Orders

*Only shown if your business takes orders.*

New orders appear at the top **instantly** with a beep and a pop-up "🛒 New order received!".

Each order card shows: order number, status, payment, customer name and number, items, delivery address or
"Pickup", notes, and the total.

**What to do with an order:**

| Order status | What it means | Your action |
|---|---|---|
| **New** (yellow) | Customer chose UPI/online and has not paid yet | Check your bank/UPI app. When the money arrives tap **✅ Payment received**. Never trust payment screenshots – check the bank. Online (Razorpay) payments confirm automatically. |
| **Confirmed** | Paid, or Cash on delivery | Tap **👨‍🍳 Preparing** when you start packing |
| **Preparing** | Being packed / cooked | Tap **🚚 Out for delivery** (or **✅ Ready for pickup**) |
| **Out for delivery / Ready** | On the way / waiting at the counter | Tap **📦 Delivered / Collected** when done |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled by you | – |

Every tap sends the customer a WhatsApp update automatically – e.g. *"🚚 Your order #1024 is out for delivery.
Please keep ₹450 ready."*

* **Cancel order** asks for a reason (optional) – it is sent to the customer.
* Tabs: *In progress*, *Awaiting payment*, *Delivered*, *Cancelled*, *All*. Search by name, phone or order number.
* **Export CSV** downloads all orders for Excel / accounting.
* "Show test orders" shows orders you placed yourself in **Test the bot**.

## 6. Appointments

*Only shown if your business takes bookings.*

Shows bookings for the selected day (or 7 / 30 days). Use ← → to move between days and **Today** to come back.

Each booking shows the time, customer, service, staff member, the customer's answers to your questions
(e.g. *Which tooth: Upper · Symptom: Swelling*), and status.

| Button | When to use | What happens automatically |
|---|---|---|
| **✅ Visit done** | After the customer's visit. You can type a prescription or note | Customer gets your aftercare message + note on WhatsApp; a "How are you feeling?" message is scheduled (e.g. after 3 days); a recall is scheduled if the service has one (e.g. 6-month cleaning) |
| **Confirm** | You called and confirmed | Status changes to Confirmed (customers can also confirm themselves from the reminder) |
| **Move** | Change the date/time | Customer gets "Your appointment has been moved to…"; reminders move too |
| **No-show** | The customer didn't come | Reminders stop; counts in "No-shows" on Home |
| **Cancel** | You must cancel | Customer is told (with your reason) and invited to book again |

**+ New appointment** – for phone calls and walk-ins: enter name and WhatsApp number, choose service, staff (or any),
date, then tap a free time. Leave "Send WhatsApp confirmation" on so the customer also gets reminders.

**Reminders are automatic.** By default 1 day and 2 hours before. The customer can tap *I'll be there*,
*Reschedule* or *Cancel* right in the reminder. Change reminder times in **Settings → Appointments**.

**After a visit (the "care loop")**

1. You tap **Visit done** → aftercare/prescription sent.
2. A few days later: *"How are you feeling now?"* with buttons 😊 Better / 😐 Same / 😟 Not good.
3. 😊 → the customer is asked for a **Google review** (only happy customers are asked).
4. 😐 or 😟 → you get an alert and the chat appears in **Chats** as *Needs reply*. Call or message them.
5. When the recall time comes (e.g. 6 months for cleaning) → *"It's time for your cleaning. Book now?"*

## 7. Chats

Every WhatsApp conversation, as in WhatsApp itself.

* **🔴 Waiting for reply** tab – customers who asked for a person, sent a photo/voice note, or reported a problem.
* Click a chat to read it. Bot messages show 🤖, yours show your name.
* **Type a reply and press Send.** The bot **pauses** for that customer so it doesn't interrupt you.
* **▶ Hand back to bot** when you are done (it also resumes on its own after the pause time, default 12 hours,
  or when the customer types *menu*).
* **✓ Mark handled** removes the red flag without replying.
* **📎 Open attachment** shows photos, prescriptions, documents or voice notes the customer sent.
* **"More than 24 hours…" message** – WhatsApp only lets businesses send normal messages within 24 hours of the
  customer's last message. Wait for the customer to message again (or call them).

## 8. Customers

Everyone who has messaged you is saved automatically with name, number, number of orders/visits and last message.

* **Search** by name or phone, **filter by tag**.
* **Edit**: name, tags (e.g. *vip, diabetic, wholesale*), address, private notes.
* **Receives reminders & updates** – switched off when the customer sends STOP.
* **Agreed to receive offers** – switch on only if the customer agreed (needed for broadcasts).
* **Delete data** – use when a customer asks you to delete their information (privacy law).
* **Import CSV** – bring your existing customers/patients from Excel. Columns:
  `phone, name, tags, notes, last_visit, service, marketing_opt_in`. If you fill *last_visit* (e.g. 2026-05-20) and
  *service* (exact service name with a recall period), recall messages are scheduled automatically – a great way
  to bring old patients back.
* **Export** – download all customers.

## 9. Enquiries

Details customers submitted through the *Send enquiry* button (for example: what they need, budget, location).
Change the status (New → Contacted → Won / Lost), add notes, export to Excel.
Change the questions in **Settings → Enquiry form**.

## 10. Products

Your catalogue for ordering.

* **+ Add product**: name (short – WhatsApp shows about 24 characters), price, category, unit (kg, pack, plate…),
  description, optional photo link, sort order.
* **In stock** switch – turn off and the item disappears from the bot instantly. Do this whenever something runs out.
* **Visible** switch – hide an item without deleting it.
* **Import CSV** – in Excel make columns `name, price, category, unit, description, in_stock, image_url, sku`, save
  as CSV and upload. Same names are updated, new names are added. Tick "Replace the whole catalogue" to start fresh.
* **Export** – download your catalogue (edit in Excel and import back).

Tips: start with your top 50–100 items; use clear categories (Staples, Dairy, Snacks…); keep prices current.

## 11. Services & staff

*For bookings.*

**Services tab** – name, duration (minutes), price (shown to customers; 0 = free), category/tag,
**aftercare message** (sent after the visit), **recall after (days)** (e.g. 180 for 6-month cleaning, 30 for a
monthly haircut), and whether customers can book it.

**Staff tab** (Doctors / Stylists / Trainers…) – name, title, WhatsApp number (gets an alert for each booking),
which services they do (none ticked = all), and working hours (business hours, or their own).

**Holidays & leave tab** – add festival holidays (whole business) or a staff member's leave. No slots are offered
on those days.

## 12. FAQs

Questions the bot answers instantly: timings, fees, location, parking, home delivery area, etc.

* **Question** – what customers ask (keep the first 24 characters meaningful – that shows in the menu).
* **Answer** – your reply. Use `*bold*` and line breaks.
* **Keywords** – words that trigger this answer when a customer **types** a question, separated by commas:
  `fees, charges, price, kitna`. Add Hindi/Hinglish words your customers use.

## 12b. AI assistant (optional)

Menu → **AI assistant**. Turn it on and your bot answers *any* question from your own information – in the
customer's language (English, Hindi, Hinglish, Gujarati…). It also understands typed orders ("2 kilo aata aur 1 maggi")
and booking requests.

* **AI engine** – choose **Claude**, **OpenAI (ChatGPT)** or **Google Gemini** and paste your API key; everything
  else works the same with all three. You can switch anytime.
* **Knowledge base** – add your price list, menu, policies, doctor profiles, delivery areas, offers: paste text,
  upload a PDF/TXT/CSV, or import a page of your website. Switch a source off when it's outdated.
* **Try a question** – type what a customer would ask and see the answer before customers do.
* **Settings** – tone (friendly / formal / short), assistant name, extra instructions ("always mention free delivery above ₹300").
* It never invents prices or policies; orders and bookings still need the customer to confirm; complaints and
  medical/legal questions go to your team.

## 12c. Menu & quick replies

Menu → **Menu & quick replies**. Add your own buttons to the WhatsApp menu – and automatic replies when customers
type certain words. Each button can send: a text, a link button (website, form, payment page), an image (offer
poster), a document (price-list PDF), a map location, or start ordering / booking / the enquiry form.
Keywords like *price list, rate, bhav* trigger the same reply when typed.

## 12d. Integrations

Menu → **Integrations**. Every new lead, order, booking, handoff and feedback can be sent automatically to
**Google Sheets**, **HubSpot**, **Airtable**, or any tool through **n8n / Make / Zapier** (webhook). Fill in the
details, **Save**, then **Send test**. The delivery log shows every send; failed sends are retried automatically.

## 13. Bot messages

Change **any** word the bot says.

* Tabs: General, Ordering, Booking, Enquiry. Or use **Search all messages**.
* Edit the text and click **Save**. "customised" marks your changes; **Reset** goes back to the original.
* Keep words in `{curly braces}` – they are filled automatically (business name, total, date, time…).
* **Button labels must be 20 characters or less** (24 for menu items). A red warning appears if too long.
* Use WhatsApp formatting: `*bold*`, `_italic_`, emojis.
* Choose the **language** at the top to edit the Hindi, Gujarati, Hinglish or English version of every text.
* The main language is chosen in **Settings → Business → Bot language**. In **Settings → Languages** you can let every
  customer pick their own language (they can also type *language* anytime).

## 14. Test the bot

A phone on your screen where you chat with your own bot **exactly like a customer** – before going live and
after every change.

* Type messages or tap the buttons. 📍 sends a test location, 📷 a test photo.
* **⏩ Fast-forward time** – sends your test bookings' reminders, check-ins and recalls immediately, so you can see
  the full journey in one minute.
* **🗑 Reset test chat** – start fresh (also deletes test orders/bookings).
* Test orders and bookings are marked **Test** and hidden from your real numbers.

## 15. Broadcasts

Send one approved template (offer, festival wishes, new service, holiday notice) to many customers.

1. Choose an **approved template** (create it first – see §16 or ask your developer).
2. Fill the template values. Use `{name}` for the customer's first name. Separate values with `|`.
3. Choose who: everyone, or only customers with a tag.
4. Keep **"Only customers who agreed to receive offers"** ON for promotions.
5. Click **Send**. Progress appears in History.

⚠️ Only send to people who agreed. Unwanted messages get reported and WhatsApp can limit or block your number.
Meta charges per marketing message.

## 16. Message templates

WhatsApp lets the bot reply freely for **24 hours** after a customer writes. Reminders, check-ins, recalls and late
order updates often happen later – those must use **templates pre-approved by Meta** (usually approved within
minutes to a day).

* Click **Submit all to Meta** once (WhatsApp must be connected). Five recommended templates are created and linked:
  owner alerts, order updates, appointment reminders, after-visit check-in, recall.
* The badge shows the status: **Approved** ✅, Pending, or Rejected.
* The bot automatically uses a template only when needed – you don't have to do anything else.

## 18. Your daily & weekly routine

**Every day (5 minutes)**
- Open the dashboard in the morning; tap once to enable sound.
- Clear **Chats → Waiting for reply**.
- Move orders forward / mark **Visit done** after each appointment.
- Switch off **In stock** for anything that ran out.

**Every week**
- Look at the Home numbers: orders, no-shows, new customers.
- Read a few chats – are customers getting stuck? Improve FAQs or bot messages.
- Add new FAQs for questions people keep asking.

**Every month**
- Review prices and services. Import any new customers. Plan one broadcast to opted-in customers (not more).

## 19. What your customers see

**Shop example:** Hi → [Order now] → choose *Dairy* → *Amul Milk* → tap **2** → [Checkout] → "Deliver to this
address?" [Yes] → [Cash on delivery] → *"🎉 Order #1024 confirmed! … Total ₹66 … Expected: 30-45 minutes"*.
Later: *"🚚 Your order #1024 is out for delivery."*

**Clinic example:** Hi → [Book appointment] → *Toothache* → *Which tooth?* [Upper] → *Feeling?* [Swelling] →
[Tomorrow] → [10:30 AM] → [For Neha] → [✅ Confirm] → *"✅ Booked! … 📍 address + map"*. Day before:
*"⏰ Reminder … [I'll be there] [Reschedule] [Cancel]"*. After the visit: prescription & care tips. 3 days later:
*"How are you feeling?"* → review request. 6 months later: *"Your cleaning is due. [Book now]"*.

Customers can always type **menu** to start again, **cancel** to stop what they are doing, **talk to someone** for
a person, and **STOP** to stop receiving messages.

## 20. Rules of WhatsApp

1. **Use a separate number** for the bot – it cannot be on the normal WhatsApp app at the same time.
2. **24-hour window** – free replies only within 24 hours of the customer's last message; after that only templates.
3. **Consent** – only send offers to people who agreed. Always honour STOP (the bot does this automatically).
4. **Quality rating** – blocks and reports lower it; low quality limits your messaging.
5. **Restricted goods** – some products (prescription medicines, alcohol, tobacco…) have extra rules.
6. **Costs** – customer-started conversations are mostly free/cheap; templates (especially marketing) are charged by
   Meta per message. Your developer can show current rates.

## 21. Questions & answers

**The bot isn't replying to a customer.** Open the chat: if it says *Bot paused*, tap **Hand back to bot**. The
customer can also type *menu*. If nobody gets replies, check Settings → WhatsApp → **Test connection** and call your
developer.

**I changed a price but the customer's cart shows the old one.** Carts are re-priced at checkout – the customer always
pays the current price.

**A customer paid by UPI but the order still says New.** Check your bank app; if the money is there, tap
**Payment received**.

**Two people booked the same time?** Not possible – the system blocks double bookings, even if two people tap at the
same second.

**Can I take the day off?** Add it in **Services & staff → Holidays & leave**. No slots will be offered.

**Can I use it in Hindi?** Choose *Hinglish* in Settings → Business, and edit any message in **Bot messages** – you
can write in Hindi script too.

**Where are my reports?** Export orders, appointments, customers and enquiries to Excel from each page.

**Is my data safe?** Passwords and WhatsApp keys are encrypted. Each business only sees its own data. Ask your
developer about daily backups.
