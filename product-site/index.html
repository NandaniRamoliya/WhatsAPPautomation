FAQ

Questions business owners ask

Is this the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. Replix uses Meta's official WhatsApp Business (Cloud) API – no unofficial tools that get numbers banned.

Can I keep my current WhatsApp number?

Usually yes. Many WhatsApp Business app numbers can also connect to the API, so you can keep replying from your phone and the bot goes quiet in that chat. Otherwise we use a new number.

What will I pay apart from the setup?

WhatsApp (Meta) charges for template messages sent after 24 hours; replies within 24 hours of a customer's message are free. Hosting is a small monthly cost. The optional AI runs on your own AI account, roughly ₹0.5–2 per AI reply.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Hinglish, Hindi and Gujarati are built in, and customers choose their language. The AI replies in whatever language the customer writes.

Can my staff reply to customers themselves?

Yes. "Talk to a person" hands the chat to your team in a shared inbox or on your phone, and the bot pauses for that customer.

Who owns the data?

You do. Replix runs in your own accounts, and you can export or delete customer data any time.

Does it work on Instagram or my website?

Replix is built for WhatsApp first. The engine is channel-ready, so a website chat or other channels can be added – ask us about your case.
